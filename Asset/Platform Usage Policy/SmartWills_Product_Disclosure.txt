3. What is a Will?
3. 什麼是遺囑
A Will is a unilateral, formal legal statement made by a testator, effective upon death, to dispose of his/her lawful property. Under the Civil Code of Taiwan, there are 5 statutory forms of will. A valid will allows you to arrange asset distribution instead of statutory succession.
遺囑係遺囑人依法定方式，於死亡後發生效力、處分自己合法財產之單獨法律行為。依台灣《民法》第1189條，遺囑分為自書遺囑、公證遺囑、密封遺囑、代筆遺囑、口授遺囑五種；有效遺囑可以優先於法定繼承安排財產分配。

4. Who is eligible to make a Will?
4. 誰可以訂立遺囑
Any natural person who meets all the following requirements may make a will:
符合以下全部條件之自然人，具有遺囑能力，可以訂立遺囑（民法第1186條）：
• Has testamentary capacity, sound mind, not subject to guardianship declaration;
• 具有遺囑能力、意識清楚、未受監護宣告；
• Aged 16 years old or above. Minors aged 16+ do not need consent from legal representative;
• 年滿16歲；滿16歲之限制行為能力人，無須法定代理人同意即可立遺囑；
• Able to understand the nature and legal consequence of making a will.
• 能夠理解訂立遺囑之意義與法律效果。

9. What are the inherent legal risks in making a will?
9. 訂立遺囑本身固有的法律風險
9.1 A will cannot override designated beneficiary arrangements under insurance contracts, annuity or other financial products. Such assets shall be paid to the designated beneficiary according to the relevant contract terms.
9.1 遺囑不能變更保險、年金或其他金融契約已指定受益人之安排，該類資產依契約約定給付指定受益人。
9.2 If a beneficiary is a minor at the time of your death, the minor’s share shall be managed by his/her guardian in accordance with law until the minor reaches the age of 20.
9.2 若受益人於您身故時為未成年人，該遺產份額依法由監護人管理，直至其滿20歲。
9.3 Compulsory shares (特留分) protected by law cannot be deprived. The compulsory share rules are set out in Art.1223 Civil Code of Taiwan. Any disposition violating compulsory share may be subject to abatement.
9.3 遺囑不得侵害法定繼承人之特留分（民法第1223條）：直系血親卑親屬、父母、配偶之特留分為應繼分1/2；祖父母、兄弟姊妹之特留分為應繼分1/3；侵害特留分部分，繼承人得主張扣減。
9.4 Nuncupative (oral) will is only valid when the testator is in imminent danger. It automatically lapses once the danger ends and the testator is able to make other forms of will.
9.4 口授遺囑僅限生命危急或特殊情形、無法使用其他方式時才可以使用；危險解除後，口授遺囑自動失其效力。

11. Who can be my Will Executor?
11. 誰可以擔任遺囑執行人
Will executor must be a person with full capacity for civil conduct, who accepts the appointment and is capable of estate administration. Legal persons or trust institutions may also act as executor.
遺囑執行人必須是具完全行為能力人、同意接受委任、有能力管理遺產；法人、信託機構也可擔任遺囑執行人。

13. Who can be my witness?
13. 誰可以擔任遺囑見證人（適用代筆、公證、密封遺囑）
Witnesses must be natural persons with full capacity for civil conduct. The following persons are disqualified: minors, persons under guardianship/assistance declaration, heirs, beneficiaries, their spouses and lineal relatives. Attested will (代筆遺囑) requires at least THREE witnesses.
見證人必須是具完全行為能力之自然人；未成年人、受監護/輔助宣告人、繼承人、受遺贈人及其配偶、直系血親，不得擔任見證人（民法第1198條）。代筆遺囑依法需要至少3位見證人。

15. What is meant by “Last Will & Testament”?
15. 「最後遺囑」之意義
A later valid will revokes inconsistent provisions of an earlier will. Where two valid wills conflict, the later one prevails. Notarized will does not enjoy priority merely by reason of notarization.
後訂立之有效遺囑，與前遺囑抵觸部分，視為撤回前遺囑；數份有效遺囑內容抵觸時，以後成立者為準。公證遺囑不具優先效力，不以公證與否判斷優先順位。
